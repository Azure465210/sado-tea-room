Sado Tea Room
An independent, non-commercial educational tea ceremony project.

Shizue-sensei and the tea room are original to this project.
The guest instructor models and dialogue are unofficial fan interpretations.
Character rights remain with their respective creators.

  https://aivoice.jp/character/kotonoha/
- Zundamon, Shikoku Metan, Tohoku Zunko, Tohoku Kiritan and Tohoku Itako.
  https://zunko.jp/guideline.html
- Kasukabe Tsumugi.
  https://tsumugi-official.studio.site/rule
